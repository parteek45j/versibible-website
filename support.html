Support

Get help with VersiBible.

Questions, bug reports, or feedback: email support@versibible.com. A real person reads every message.

What to include

Your device model, iOS version, and the app version, plus a short description or screenshot of what happened.

Subscriptions and billing

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple. To cancel or change your plan, open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before a free trial or billing period ends to avoid the next charge. If you reinstall the app or switch devices, tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

You can delete your account and everything synced to it from the You tab inside the app (Delete account), or email us and we'll remove it for you. See the privacy summary for what the app stores.
